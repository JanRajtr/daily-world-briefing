Healthy and tasty menu

  1. Breakfast: Czech-style Whole Grain Waffles with Fresh Berries and Yogurt

    Mix 1 cup whole wheat flour, 2 tablespoons sugar, 2 teaspoons baking powder, and 1/4 teaspoon salt. In a separate bowl, whisk 1 large egg, 1 cup milk, and 2 tablespoons melted butter. Combine wet and dry ingredients, then cook in a waffle iron at medium heat for 5 minutes. Serve with 1/2 cup mixed berries, 6 ounces plain yogurt, and a drizzle of honey.

  2. Lunch: Grilled Chicken and Vegetable Wrap

    Preheat a grill or grill pan to medium-high heat. Season 4 ounces boneless chicken breast with salt, pepper, and 1 tablespoon olive oil. Grill for 6-7 minutes per side, or until cooked through. Meanwhile, grill 1/2 cup sliced bell peppers and 1/2 cup sliced zucchini for 3-4 minutes per side. Warm a whole wheat tortilla in the microwave for 20 seconds. Fill with the grilled chicken, vegetables, 1/4 cup hummus, and mixed greens.

  3. Snack: Apple and Almond Butter Slices

    Spread 2 tablespoons almond butter onto 1 medium sliced apple. Sprinkle with a pinch of cinnamon and serve.

  4. Dinner: Baked Salmon with Roasted Carrots and Quinoa

    Preheat the oven to 400°F (200°C). Season 6 ounces salmon fillet with salt, pepper, and 1 tablespoon olive oil. Bake for 12-15 minutes, or until cooked through. Toss 2 medium carrots with 1 tablespoon olive oil, salt, and pepper. Roast in the oven for 20-25 minutes, or until tender. Cook 1/2 cup quinoa according to package instructions. Serve with the baked salmon and roasted carrots.
