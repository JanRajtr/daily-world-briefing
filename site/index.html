Healthy and tasty menu

  1. Breakfast: Whole Grain Waffles with Fresh Berries and Yogurt

    Ingredients: 1 cup whole wheat flour, 2 tablespoons sugar, 2 teaspoons baking powder, 1/4 teaspoon salt, 1 large egg, 1 cup milk, 2 tablespoons melted butter, 1 cup mixed berries, 6 ounces yogurt. Instructions: Preheat waffle iron to medium-high heat. In a bowl, whisk together flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt. In another bowl, whisk together egg, milk, and melted butter. Combine wet and dry ingredients, stir until just combined. Cook waffles for 5-7 minutes or until golden brown. Serve with fresh berries and yogurt.

  2. Lunch: Grilled Chicken and Vegetable Wrap

    Ingredients: 1 boneless, skinless chicken breast, 1/2 cup sliced cucumber, 1/2 cup sliced bell peppers, 1/4 cup sliced red onion, 1 tablespoon hummus, 1 whole wheat tortilla. Instructions: Preheat grill to medium-high heat. Grill chicken for 5-6 minutes per side or until cooked through. Slice chicken into strips. Spread hummus on tortilla, layer with grilled chicken, cucumber, bell peppers, and red onion. Roll up wrap and slice in half.

  3. Snack: Apple and Almond Butter Slice

    Ingredients: 1 medium apple, 2 tablespoons almond butter. Instructions: Slice apple into thin rounds. Spread 1 tablespoon almond butter on each apple slice. Serve and enjoy.

  4. Dinner: Baked Salmon with Roasted Vegetables

    Ingredients: 4 salmon fillets, 2 tablespoons olive oil, 1 large sweet potato, 1 large carrot, 1 large Brussels sprouts. Instructions: Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C). Line a baking sheet with parchment paper. Place salmon fillets on baking sheet, drizzle with 1 tablespoon olive oil. Roast in oven for 12-15 minutes or until cooked through. Toss sweet potato, carrot, and Brussels sprouts with remaining 1 tablespoon olive oil, salt, and pepper on a separate baking sheet. Roast in oven for 20-25 minutes or until tender.
