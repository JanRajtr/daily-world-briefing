Healthy and tasty menu

  1. Breakfast: Summer Fruit Oat Bowl

    In a pot, bring 250ml of water to a boil, then reduce heat to low and add 50g of rolled oats. Cook, stirring occasionally, for 5-7 minutes or until the oats have absorbed most of the water and have a creamy consistency. Top with 100g of mixed summer fruits (such as blueberries, strawberries, raspberries), 1 tablespoon of honey, and a sprinkle of chopped almonds.

  2. Lunch: Grilled Chicken and Vegetable Wrap

    Preheat a grill or grill pan to medium-high heat. Season 100g of boneless, skinless chicken breast with salt, pepper, and your favorite herbs. Grill the chicken for 5-6 minutes per side, or until cooked through. Meanwhile, grill 1/2 bell pepper and 1/2 zucchini, sliced, for 3-4 minutes per side. Warm a whole wheat tortilla, then fill with the grilled chicken, vegetables, 1 tablespoon of hummus, and a handful of mixed greens.

  3. Snack: Carrot and Apple Slices with Peanut Butter

    Cut 2 medium carrots and 1 medium apple into slices. Serve with 2 tablespoons of peanut butter for a satisfying and healthy snack.

  4. Dinner: Baked Salmon with Roasted Vegetables

    Preheat the oven to 200°C. Season 120g of salmon fillet with salt, pepper, and lemon juice. Place on a baking sheet lined with parchment paper and bake for 12-15 minutes, or until cooked through. Toss 1 medium sweet potato, peeled and cubed, 1 medium broccoli crown, and 1 medium red onion, sliced, with 1 tablespoon of olive oil, salt, and pepper. Spread on a baking sheet and roast in the oven for 20-25 minutes, or until the vegetables are tender and lightly browned.
